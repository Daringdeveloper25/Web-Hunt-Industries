About Web Hunt Industries

I started Web Hunt Industries because I thought it would be a fun and unique way to pursue my passion for web design while also making some money.
It all started when a small business owner in a Facebook group needed some help with her website and she and I connected and collaborated on it to improve the overall design, layout and functionality.
After that, I started to come up with the idea for the business name, Web Hunt Industries, which is both a play on my name (Jeb Hunt) and a description of what I do, which is to search for the best ways to display things on the web.
For all of my clients, I hope to provide visually pleasing and user-friendly experiences for all of their websites.
My overall vision for the business is to be something that people can look to in their desrire for great websites at low rates.


Meet the Developer

My name is Jeb Hunt, and I am the man behind Web Hunt Industries.
As far back as I can remember, I have always been interested in technology, and have known for a long time that I have wanted a career in that field.
I originally studied Computer Game Design at George Mason University, but became interested in web design while studying that major.
In the summer of 2025, I enrolled in a summer program called the Global Career Accelerator, studied the Web Design and Development path in that program, and that is where I really fell in love with web design. Eventually, I decided to start a business as a freelancer and named it Web Hunt Industries, and that is how the business came to be! The reason I like web design so much is because I get to basically use my creativity and turn it into a functioning website, and I think that is the best part of the job!
